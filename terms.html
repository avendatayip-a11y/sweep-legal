What Sweep is

Sweep is a guided checking tool. It runs three scans your iPhone is genuinely capable of — listing devices on the Wi-Fi network you are connected to, brightening the camera feed so faint infrared light and bright reflections are easier to see, and listing nearby Bluetooth devices that are broadcasting — and it walks you through checking a room by hand.

Sweep is not a detector and cannot tell you a room is safe. It does not identify hidden cameras, and it never reports that a room is clear. A camera that records to a memory card joins no network and emits no signal, so no app on any phone can rule one out. Sweep reports only what it actually observed and leaves the judgement to you.

Use it lawfully and sensibly

  • Scan only networks you are authorised to use — the Wi-Fi of a place you are staying in or your own home. Scanning networks you have no right to access may be unlawful where you are.
  • Do not rely on Sweep as your only safeguard, and do not rely on it in situations where being wrong would cause you harm.
  • If you find something you believe is a hidden camera, contact the local police. Sweep is not a substitute for law enforcement or for a professional inspection.
