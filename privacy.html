Privacy Policy

Sweep: Hidden Camera Check · Last updated 7 August 2026

Sweep is designed so that there is almost nothing to collect. The scans run on your iPhone and their results stay on your iPhone. This page describes exactly what happens to data, including the parts most privacy policies leave vague.

What Sweep does not collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No camera images. The infrared view and the lens sweep process the live camera feed on the device and never record, save or transmit it.
  • No network scan results. The list of devices found on your Wi-Fi — their addresses, open ports and advertised names — is held in memory while you look at it and is discarded when the scan is replaced or the app closes.
  • No Bluetooth data. Nearby device names and signal strengths are shown live and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • No location data. Sweep does not request location access.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.

What is collected

Purchase history only, and only if you buy a subscription. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, Inc., which manages subscription state so the app knows whether your subscription is active.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier it generates for the installation, the products you purchased, and the status of those purchases. It does not receive your name, your email address, your scan results or anything from your camera. This data is used only to keep your subscription working — for example to restore it after you reinstall the app — and is not used for advertising or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

RevenueCat's own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's handling of the payment itself is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions and why they are asked for

  • Local Network — to list the devices that answer on the Wi-Fi network you are connected to. iOS requires your permission for this. Nothing is sent off the device; the scan is your iPhone contacting other devices on the same network.
  • Camera — for the infrared view and the lens sweep. The frames are processed and drawn on screen, then discarded.
  • Bluetooth — to list nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices that are broadcasting. Sweep never connects to them.

Each permission can be withdrawn at any time in Settings. The feature that needs it stops working; nothing else changes.

Diagnostic log

Sweep keeps a short technical log on the device to help diagnose problems — scan timings, error messages and similar. It stays on your iPhone, is not sent anywhere automatically, and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Sweep is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
